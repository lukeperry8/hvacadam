Skilled Trades Ontario Trade Code 313D

STO Training Standards, Instrument Guide & Pass Criteria

Detailed breakdown of all 8 Skill Sets, mandatory specialty instruments, safety compliance (CSA B52, ODP Reg. 463/10), and sign-off benchmarks.

Mandatory Specialty Instruments
  • Digital Manifolds & Thermocouples: Superheat & subcooling calculations.
  • Electronic Micron Gauges: Deep vacuum verification below 500 microns.
  • Electronic Leak Detectors: Heated diode / ultrasonic refrigerant leak checks.
  • Clamp Meters & DMMs: Voltage, resistance, and compressor starting amperage.
  • Anemometers & Manometers: Airflow CFM and external static pressure (ESP).
Regulatory Compliance & Codes
  • CSA B52: Mechanical Refrigeration Code for pressure testing and relief systems.
  • Ontario Reg. 463/10 (ODP): Mandatory certification for recovering and handling fluorinated refrigerants.
  • OHSA & CSA Z462: Workplace safety, WHMIS, and electrical lock-out/tag-out (LOTO).
